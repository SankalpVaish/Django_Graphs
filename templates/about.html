About

An end-to-end machine learning workbench

Graphs is a learning-focused tool for working through the full machine learning workflow — from a raw CSV to a trained, evaluated model.

Upload a dataset, explore it visually, and train models with configurable preprocessing. Every step is designed to show what happened and why it matters, rather than hiding the workflow behind a single button.

What you can do

  • Exploratory analysis Scatter, line, bar and histogram plots across any pair of columns.
  • Outlier handling Spot outliers on a boxplot and clip them to the column mean in one click.
  • Correlation profiling See how strongly numerical features move together before modelling.
  • Configurable preprocessing Choose your scaler and train/test split, then compare the effect.
  • Model comparison Six classification and six regression algorithms, scored side by side.
  • Readable diagnostics Learning curves, complexity curves and feature importance, explained.
